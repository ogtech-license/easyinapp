Child Safety Standards for Easy In

Developer: OGTech
App: Easy In

At OGTech, protecting safety and preventing child exploitation is a top priority. Easy In strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) of any kind.

Users can report violations directly in-app or by contacting our safety team. We actively moderate content, remove non-compliant material immediately, and report illegal activities to relevant law enforcement agencies and national authorities (such as NCMEC).

Easy In Application
Easy In is a robust mobile application for the end-user with a friendly interface and backed Control Panel, it is also secure, convenient & easy way to control and manage your facility access for residents and visitors.

Easy In has 4 modules:

  • Gate Access.
  • Facility Management.
  • Online Payment.
  • Explore.
