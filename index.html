Child Safety Standards Policy for Easy In

Developer: OGTech
Application: Easy In
Designated Safety Contact Email: license@ogtech.com

1. Zero Tolerance Policy against CSAE and CSAM

At OGTech, protecting user safety—especially children's safety—is a top priority. The Easy In application strictly prohibits any creation, upload, transmission, distribution, host, or sharing of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form.

2. Reporting Mechanism & Contact Information

Users can report potential violations or harmful content directly within the Easy In app or by emailing our designated child safety team at license@ogtech.com. All child safety reports are treated with extreme urgency and prioritized for immediate review.

3. Moderation, Removal & Account Enforcement

Our dedicated safety team actively monitors and moderates reported content. Upon receiving a report:

  • Any reported content violating CSAE/CSAM policies is immediately disabled and removed.
  • Accounts responsible for distributing or attempting to distribute CSAE/CSAM content are permanently banned immediately without notice.

4. Law Enforcement & Authority Reporting

OGTech fully cooperates with global law enforcement agencies and relevant national authorities. Any confirmed instances of CSAM or CSAE will be promptly reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and applicable legal authorities.
